      Head-to-Head Matchup

      Pick two franchises and a venue. Output is win probability and projected score, derived from each team's ORACLE-predicted 2026 wins. Methodology below.

      Methodology. Each team's ORACLE-predicted wins are converted to a per-game strength rating in points (rating = (pred_wins − 8.5) × 1.5). The projected spread from Team A's perspective is (rA − rB) + home-field advantage, with HFA = +2.5 points at home and 0 on a neutral field. Win probability is a normal CDF on the spread with σ = 13.86 (the conventional NFL game volatility). Projected total uses the league-average 44.5 points; individual team scores split that total around the spread. Treat single-game outputs as a probability-weighted estimate, not a precise prediction — NFL games carry ±10 points of real-world noise per side.
